Mathematical Definition: The loading values reported here represent scaled component loadings (l_ij = v_ij × √λ_i, where v_ij is the eigenvector component and λ_i is the eigenvalue). Under standardized features, scaled component loadings approximate feature-component associations; however, depending on variance divisor conventions (N vs N-1), they are formally scaled component loadings rather than exact sample Pearson correlations. They quantify the magnitude and direction of association, distinct from normalized eigenvector weights whose sum of squares equals 1.

Analytical cohort & volume effects: The PCA model is fitted on 774 administrative districts using log1p-transformed and standardized counts ($\log(1+x)$) across 131 leaf and component crime categories.
