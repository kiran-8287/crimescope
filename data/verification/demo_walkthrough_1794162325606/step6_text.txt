Inertia is the within-cluster sum of squared distances to each centroid (the K-means objective). In general this objective is non-increasing as k rises, but independently fitted local K-means runs need not follow that ordering. Either way it is not a goodness-of-fit score, and a lower objective alone does not establish an optimal k. The saved run provides k = 3, 4 and 5; k = 3 is the selected configuration, not a proven optimum.
