Analytical cohort & volume effects: The PCA is fitted on 774 administrative districts using log1p-transformed counts standardised per feature (population standard deviation, ddof = 0) across 131 crime features. Because population denominators are unavailable, PC1 primarily reflects overall reporting volume (Pearson r ≈ −0.88 with log-volume, ≈ −0.71 with raw volume). The two saved components retain 20.48% of the total feature variance, so positions in this plot summarise partial profile structure — they are not a measure of community safety and are not a complete similarity map.
