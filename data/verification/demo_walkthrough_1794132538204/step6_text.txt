Smallest cluster in this configuration: 168 units

With k=3, the smallest cluster (Cluster 2, n=168) is separated in the standardized space by its highest values in Offences against State › Other offences against State, Offences affecting Human Body › Terrorist act, Offences against State › Offences against State (a+b) and its lowest values in Murder, Theft › Vehicle Theft, Offences against Women and Child › Cruelty by Husband or Relatives. The smallest cluster is not necessarily the most elevated one: at k=3 the smallest group (C2, n=168) is distinguished by low values on the largest volume offences rather than high ones. Because each feature is standardized on its own national distribution, categories with near-zero national variance can produce large standardized values from very small recorded counts. Such separations therefore reflect a position in the transformed feature space rather than overall crime level, and do not by themselves indicate a distinct policy or demographic profile.
