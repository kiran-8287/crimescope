The three flag groups are mutually exclusive and, together, exhaustive — every saved flag falls in exactly one of them. Rare (19) + higher-count (1,307) + intermediate (673) = 1,999, which equals the 1,999 saved flags. The rare predicate tests the count and |z| only; it does not itself measure the share of zero-reporting districts, so that share is not claimed here.

Why a single case can produce a z-score of ~27.78

Saved flags use the sample standard deviation (n−1 denominator) computed per category across the 2024 administrative-district cohort. In a sparse category a single positive count makes the cohort variance very small, so the resulting z-score is large and mathematically expected rather than evidence of high crime volume.
