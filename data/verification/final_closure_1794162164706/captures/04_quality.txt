What completeness means: “Non-null supplied cells” measures the fraction of non-null values among the table cells that were actually supplied in the acquired source files. It does not prove that every NCRB publication, crime section or reporting unit was published or ingested, nor that reporting boundaries were identical across years. It is a supplied-cell measure, not a measure of complete publication coverage or of actual crime coverage.
What “0 documented source gaps” means: No trailing fields were absent from the trailing columns of the 144 active source files, so no cells were left zero-filled for that reason in the active 2021–2024 release. This describes the active release only; it is not evidence of perfect source data, and the historical Andhra Pradesh 2020 missing-field issues belong to the archived-scope documentation, outside this release.
