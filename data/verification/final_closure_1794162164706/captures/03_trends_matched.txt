This is an operational jurisdiction-name match: it controls membership in the comparison set but does not establish unchanged administrative boundaries, population, or coding practice. The matched-cohort change (+3.15%) uses the name-matched 2022 total as its denominator, while the annual change (+4.70%) uses the full annual 2022 official total for the same unit scope — the two denominators are different and are not interchangeable.

Annual units are every reporting unit of the selected type in that year; matched units are the subset present in both years under the same jurisdiction name; excluded units are the remainder (2022: 1 unit; 2023: 21 units). “Matched units” is not the full annual candidate pool — it is the name-matched subset, so candidate and matched counts are intentionally distinct from the annual unit count. Excluded cases are annual cases minus matched cases.

Comparability boundaries: Annual totals are shown as isolated points and never connected across the 2023–2024 legal-framework break. Only the 2022–2023 name-matched cohort is joined, and only between its own two years. 2021–2022 includes district reorganisation in Andhra Pradesh.
